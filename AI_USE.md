# Use of artificial intelligence

The AllpaNina team used AI assistants during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. This page states how, so that judges and users can weigh the work accordingly.

## What AI was used for

- **Coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic).** Wrote and revised code under the team's direction:
  - the processing scripts (`scripts/`);
  - the packaged algorithm (`allpanina/`, including region mode and the FIRMS downloader);
  - the in-browser version of the algorithm and the "Mi región" page;
  - the tests for all of the above.
